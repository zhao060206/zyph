Spring textures are traced and cropped from the site's own cherry blossom photograph `photos/2026-03-05/1_1789824696604.jpg`, with brightness/color adjustments and rotated variants. Original photograph rights remain with the photographer.

Snow grains and sunlight are generated locally in Canvas; no remote image or generation service is called at runtime.
